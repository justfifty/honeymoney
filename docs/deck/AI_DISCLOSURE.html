AI Disclosure — HoneyMoney

Team JUST50 · Updated 30 August 2026 · Companion document: Technical Architecture

1. AI inside the product

EngineRoleWhat it receives
On-deviceThe default — receipt OCR, text parsing and category classification, by Tesseract and our own parserNothing leaves the device. Zero AI tokens.
Gemini FlashOptional image extractionThe image you chose to scan.
GroqOptional phrasing of an answerSlot names, an intent, a locale. No figures, labels or free text.
Ollama (local)Either, on your own hardwareFull context — nothing leaves the machine.

The model never does arithmetic. Deterministic code computes every figure; the model only phrases it, and each number in its prose is checked against the computed set. One unrecognised figure and the whole answer is discarded for a template. The AI moves no money, executes no transactions and recommends no product — that is regulated advice, routed to a licensed directory.

2. AI in how we build it

Claude Code (Anthropic) was used throughout development. Architecture, the financial model and user-facing copy were reviewed and accepted by the team; the commit history is public and non-backdated. Claims are kept honest by scripts rather than by memory — check:ask proves the model cannot introduce a number, check:vault that no plaintext survives into a backup, check:capture that what you type is what gets stored.

3. The stack that carries it

  • GitHub — repository hosting, version control and review. No customer data.
  • Cloudflare — Pages serves an always-on static snapshot holding no personal data and proxies signed-in routes to the app server; R2 stores nightly backups encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before upload, so Cloudflare holds ciphertext.
  • PocketBase, hosted on DOM Cloud (Singapore) — one self-hosted binary providing the database, auth and migrations, and holding the household knowledge graph. Collections are superuser-only; no browser reaches them directly. Your records are stored in Singapore, not Malaysia, on a rented host reachable over the internet rather than only from our own machine.

4. Consent, and what we cannot see

AI is off until a household turns it on, per purpose, recorded append-only, withdrawable, and enforced at the call site; core budgeting stays fully usable with no AI at all. Nothing a parser proposes is saved until you confirm it, and an upload is used only for the feature you chose. Forecasts come from what you entered and can be wrong; they are educational. HoneyMoney is not a bank, lender, adviser or credit-scoring service. Backups are sealed in your browser with a passphrase we never receive. The live database is not encrypted to us — the server computes your score over it — and saying so is part of the disclosure. We sell software, not data: no brokerage, no advertising, no “anonymised insights” product.

If any statement here becomes inaccurate, we correct it. A capability left in a disclosure after it has left the code is the same defect as an undisclosed one.
