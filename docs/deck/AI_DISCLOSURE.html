AI Disclosure Statement — HoneyMoney

Team JUST50 · Updated 25 August 2026

What the AI does

Our companion “Honey” reads messy real-world input — a photographed receipt, a forwarded e-wallet screenshot, a sentence typed one-handed — and explains a household's position in plain English or Malay. It does not calculate the figures it speaks, and it does not recommend financial products.

Numbers come from code, not the model

Ask Honey runs parse → compute → narrate. A deterministic function owns every figure — the same computeHScore behind the H-Score screen, so the chat and the score cannot disagree. The model phrases those figures, and every number in its prose is checked against the computed set before display; an unrecognised number is discarded in favour of a deterministic template. Every number a household reads has been computed, never composed.

Scope

Honey answers questions about your own money. Product recommendation is regulated advice and is routed to a licensed directory. An affordability question with no price asks for the price rather than guessing one. The 3-Bucket allocation is rules-based and user-configured: the AI never moves or locks funds and executes no transactions.

Models, and what they receive

Providers are switchable per deployment, with a per-call token and cost ledger: Google Gemini Flash (multimodal, the only one doing image OCR), Groq (text insight), and Ollama (local — nothing leaves the machine; hosts open models such as DeepSeek and Qwen). A household may supply its own key; it is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before storage, never returned to a browser, and validated against the provider when saved. Extraction sends the forwarded image. Insight sends derived, minimised aggregates — never a raw ledger.

Privacy

AI features are off until a household switches them on. Consent is asked per purpose and recorded in an append-only ledger — withdrawal included — and a one-click export returns everything held. The default in-app receipt scan runs on-device OCR (Tesseract, in the browser), reads itemised line items, and contacts no model at all. Private Spendings buckets are excluded from shared household views. Data is isolated per household, settings are encrypted at rest, and backups are encrypted, held off-site, and proven restorable. There are no analytics SDKs and no data brokers, and the transaction ledger is append-only and hash-chained, so a record can be voided, never rewritten. This is alignment with PDPA 2010, not certified compliance — no third-party audit yet.

AI used to build HoneyMoney

We used AI coding assistants during development. Architecture, data model, financial logic and copy were reviewed and accepted by the team, and the commit history is public and non-backdated.

Limitations

OCR is not perfect; extraction is confirmed by the user before anything is filed. Insights are educational, not licensed financial advice. Below 8 records or 14 days, Honey declines to project rather than estimating.

If any statement here becomes inaccurate, we will correct it. A capability left in a disclosure after it has left the code is the same defect as an undisclosed one.
