HoneyMoney

Funding transparency. Spending autonomy. A personal financial wellness app — AI-supported, no tracking fatigue.

The problem

Money is the #1 source of conflict in Malaysian households, and that stress reaches work as lost productivity — an estimated ~9 lost productive days per employee per year. Budgeting apps surveil every ringgit, demand manual entry until users quit, and store data too flat to warn before a goal slips.

Our solution

HoneyMoney models a household's money as a living knowledge graph under a 3-Bucket method: Must-paid (rent, bills), a Savings auto-allocation percentage, and a private Spendings bucket where tracking stops. Because the graph knows how income flows into buckets and out to real spending, our AI companion “Honey” sees structurally when one bucket's spending velocity will push a shared goal later — and nudges before it happens, for one person, two partners, or a whole family. Capture is frictionless: forward an e-wallet or receipt screenshot to a Telegram bot, or use on-device OCR and voice that consume no AI tokens and never leave the device.

Why it's technically credible

One graph engine serves a solo earner with freelance and shop income, a couple, and a family with zero schema changes — three live personas; the demo adds one household per H-Score band. AI is swappable and optional across Google Gemini Flash, Groq and local Ollama, with a per-call token ledger — and every figure a household reads is produced by a deterministic function, not by the model. The stack is self-hostable: one PocketBase binary plus a Next.js app. Settings are encrypted at rest, household-supplied AI keys are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before storage, and backups are encrypted off-site.

Market & model

A free consumer tier grows by family referral; revenue comes from B2B2C employee financial wellness, where employers sponsor seats — no incumbent occupies the Malaysia + shared-household + cross e-wallet cell. Infrastructure cost is effectively zero, so gross margin is near-100%. Cradle CIP Spark is our non-dilutive entry.

Impact

Financial resilience for underbanked households, less money-driven conflict, healthier workplaces — mapped to SDG 1 (No Poverty), 3 (Good Health), 8 (Decent Work), the BNM financial-inclusion agenda and Malaysia's MADANI agenda.
