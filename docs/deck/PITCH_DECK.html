Relevance

The Problem We Solve

Money is the #1 source of household conflict, and that stress follows people to work — an estimated ~9 lost productive days per employee per year.

What fails today

  • Budget apps feel like partner surveillance.
  • Manual entry fatigue leads to abandonment.
  • Flat ledgers cannot forecast goal failure early.

Malaysia-specific gap

  • Multi e-wallet reality: TNG, MAE, GrabPay, ShopeePay.
  • Underbanked and mixed-income households need practical tools.
  • No incumbent owns the Malaysia + couples + cross e-wallet cell.

Technical

Our Solution: The 3-Bucket Graph Method

A living knowledge graph that converts scattered transactions into forward-looking household decisions.

3 Buckets

  • Fixed Non-Negotiables (rent, bills, essentials)
  • Future Shield (automatic savings percentage)
  • Private wallets (autonomy zone, no surveillance)

Graph intelligence

  • Links income -> buckets -> spending -> goals.
  • Detects spending velocity risk before goal failure.
  • Delivers partner-safe nudges without exposing private wallet details.
