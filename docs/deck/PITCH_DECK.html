The solution — a 3-step system
Relevance · Technical

The 3-Bucket method: transparency where it helps, privacy where it matters

On payday, income splits top-down into three buckets — so the shared money is clear and personal money stays private.

1

Must-paid

Rent, bills, essentials — hardcoded and fully transparent to both partners.

2

Savings

A set % auto-saved before any spending is tracked — pay-yourselves-first, on autopilot.

3

Spendings

Capped personal pools where tracking stops — restoring autonomy and killing spousal friction.

Why it works where budgeting apps don't

  • The user is the hero; Honey is the guide — it nudges you both, never polices either of you.
  • A hybrid of the methods people already trust: pay-yourself-first + envelope caps + zero-based intent.
