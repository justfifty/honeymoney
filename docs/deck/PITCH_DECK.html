HoneyMoney

A simple money app for couples and small businesses. See the shared money clearly, keep personal money private, and let AI help — without the tracking fatigue.

We cut money stress by replacing "watch-every-ringgit" budgeting with a simple system couples can actually stick to.
Team HoneyMoney Live: honeymoney.app
Relevance

The Problem We Solve

Money is the #1 source of household conflict, and that stress follows people to work — an estimated ~9 lost productive days per employee per year.

Why today's apps fail

  • They feel like your partner is watching every ringgit.
  • Typing in every expense is tiring, so people quit.
  • They only record the past — they can't warn you early.

The Malaysia gap

  • People use many e-wallets: TNG, MAE, GrabPay, ShopeePay.
  • Families with irregular income need simple, practical tools.
  • No app today is built for Malaysian couples across all these wallets.

Technical

Our Solution: The 3-Bucket Method

We connect all your money into one simple map, so the app can look ahead — not just record the past.

3 simple buckets

  • Must-pay: rent, bills, essentials
  • Future Shield: saves a set % automatically
  • Private wallets: personal money, kept private

What the money map does

  • Connects income → buckets → spending → goals.
  • Spots when you're spending too fast to hit a shared goal — early.
  • Nudges you both, without ever showing each other's private wallet.

Technical

Product Experience

Easy to add spending, clear insights, and your data stays on your own device.

Adding a spend is easy

  • Forward an e-wallet screenshot to a Telegram bot.
  • Or scan a receipt / just say it out loud — on your phone.
  • No bank link needed to get started.

What you get back

  • A dashboard plus six ways to see your money.
  • Spending by day, week, and month — with a forecast.
  • Works in many languages and currencies.

Technical

Architecture and AI Reliability

Stack

  • Next.js 16 PWA frontend.
  • PocketBase local-first knowledge graph data layer.
  • Telegram bot ingestion + API services.

AI you can trust

  • Can switch AI providers: Gemini Flash, Groq, or Ollama.
  • We track the cost of every AI call.
  • Data stays on the user's device — a real privacy (PDPA) story.
  • The money map gives a new, fairer way to check reliability — a base for inclusive credit.

Scalability

Why This Scales

The product scales

  • One system works for a person, a family, or a small business.
  • Ready-made templates make setup quick and easy.
  • Runs in the web browser — nothing to install.

The costs stay low

  • Data lives on the user's device, so we store very little.
  • If one AI provider fails, we switch — no lock-in.
  • We track AI cost per feature, so spending stays in control.
