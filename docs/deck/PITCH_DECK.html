The Problem

Malaysian households run money across four or five e-wallets — TNG, MAE, GrabPay, ShopeePay — plus cards and cash. Nothing adds them up.

Traditional apps answer that with manual entry and surveillance, so people burn out and stop. Tracking fatigue is the failure mode, not missing features. For couples it is worse: no shared view of what must be paid, and no private space for what shouldn't be shared.

The result is not a data problem. It is a decision problem. Households find out they overspent after the money is gone.

Only 27% of middle-income Malaysians could last six months on savings in 2025 — down from 32% a year earlier. RinggitPlus RMFLS 2025

LAYER 4

Switchable AI

Gemini Flash, Groq, or local Ollama — chosen per deployment. Cost tracked per call. No vendor lock-in.

Honey does not do the arithmetic. Every figure a user sees is computed by the same function behind the H-Score, then each number in the model's prose is checked against that allowlist — one invented figure discards the whole answer in favour of a deterministic template.

Market & Traction

Market size

8.2 million Malaysian households (DOSM, 2024), averaging 1.8 income earners each — so shared-versus-private money is the norm, not a niche. Beachhead: urban dual-income couples, where the friction is daily.

Gap

Existing tools serve one of two ends: bank apps that see only their own bank, or spreadsheets that see everything but demand discipline nobody sustains.

Where we sit

The daily-use layer across every wallet, with a couple-aware privacy model that a bank-owned app structurally cannot offer.

Evidence today

A working public build at honeymoney.app; the AI pipeline running with per-call cost tracking; a household ledger with backup and restore proven end to end. [Pilot numbers — users, weekly active, retention — to be filled from the pilot before submission.]

Business Model

Strategy & Growth

Free for households, forever: 3-bucket tracking, the Money Health Score and forecasts. That is the acquisition engine, not a trial — a budgeting tool people abandon earns nothing at any price.

Malaysia Fit & Impact

Why this matters here, and to whom

🏛️ National agenda

Household financial resilience sits squarely inside the MADANI cost-of-living agenda and Bank Negara Malaysia's financial literacy priorities. HoneyMoney is a tool for the behaviour those policies ask for, not a substitute for licensed advice.

🌍 SDG alignment

SDG 1 (No Poverty) — an emergency buffer built by rule rather than willpower. SDG 8 (Decent Work) — financial stress follows people to work; employer-sponsored seats treat it as the workplace issue it is.

🔐 Privacy as a design rule

No bank credentials are ever requested, and the default receipt scan runs on-device with Tesseract — no image leaves the phone to be read. A receipt you choose to attach is kept with its record, and you can delete it. Consistent with PDPA 2010; described as alignment, not certified compliance, because no audit has been done.

💛 Who it reaches

Free forever for households means the people under the most cost-of-living pressure are not the ones priced out. Six languages, and a phone-first PWA that installs on hardware families already own.
