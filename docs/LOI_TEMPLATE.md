## 1. Purpose and Non-Binding Intent

This Letter of Intent ("LOI") records the Parties' mutual, good-faith intention to run a **no-cost pilot** of HoneyMoney as a financial-wellness benefit for a group of the Employer's staff.

This LOI is **non-binding**. It does not create any obligation to purchase, pay, or enter into a definitive commercial agreement. Either Party may end the pilot at any time on written notice, with no penalty. It is an expression of interest to test the service and, if the pilot goes well, to discuss a paid rollout in good faith.

## 2. About HoneyMoney

HoneyMoney is an AI-powered financial-wellness benefit for employees. Participating staff (and their households) forward everyday e-wallet and spending screenshots to a private Telegram assistant. The AI organises this into a personal picture of their spending and gives proactive, plain-language money guidance — helping employees reduce financial stress, a leading driver of absenteeism and disengagement.

## 3. Scope of the Pilot

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Participants | Approximately **[20–50]** employees, enrolled voluntarily |
| Duration | **[60–90] days**, starting on or around **[Start Date]** |
| Cost to Employer | **RM 0** — fully free for the pilot period |
| Delivery | Via each participant's own smartphone (Telegram); no IT installation required |

## 4. What Each Party Provides

**The Provider (HoneyMoney) will:**
- Onboard participating employees and provide setup instructions and a short launch briefing.
- Operate the AI assistant and deliver personalised guidance to each participant throughout the pilot.
- Provide the Employer with **anonymised, aggregate** wellness insights (see Section 5).
- Give responsive support to participants and a named contact to the Employer's HR team.

**The Employer ([Company Name]) will:**
- Nominate an internal champion / HR contact to coordinate the pilot.
- Help communicate the benefit to eligible employees and invite voluntary sign-ups.
- Provide reasonable feedback at a mid-point check-in and an end-of-pilot review.
- Allow HoneyMoney to reference the Employer as a pilot participant in the competition and investor materials **only with the Employer's prior written consent**.

## 5. Data Privacy, PDPA & Anonymised Aggregates

- The Parties will handle all personal data in accordance with Malaysia's **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)**.
- Participation is **voluntary** and based on each employee's own consent. Employees may withdraw at any time and request deletion of their data.
- **Declining to take part, or withdrawing later, carries no consequence of any kind for the employee.** It will not be recorded in any employment file, communicated to a manager, or taken into account in any assessment, and the Employer will not be told who declined. Consent given to an employer is only meaningful if refusing it is genuinely free, and an employee who suspects otherwise has not consented in any sense worth relying on.
- Individual employee financial data is **private to that employee**. The Employer will **never** receive any individual's spending data, screenshots, personal financial details, or Money Health Score.
- Any insight shared with the Employer will be **anonymised and aggregated** across the participant group (for example, "68% of participants reported feeling more in control of money").
- **Aggregates are reported only for groups of 10 or more participants.** Any figure covering fewer than 10 people is withheld entirely rather than rounded, blurred, or labelled "small sample" — in a team of six, "one participant is in financial difficulty" identifies that person to everyone who can count. This applies to every breakdown, including by department, site, age band or job grade, and to combinations of them.
- The Provider will not sell participant personal data and will use it only to deliver and improve the service.

## 6. No Payment During Pilot

No fees, charges, or payment of any kind are due from the Employer to the Provider during the pilot period. Any future paid engagement would be the subject of a **separate written agreement** negotiated after the pilot.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep confidential any non-public information shared for the purposes of the pilot and use it only to run and evaluate the pilot.

## 8. Term

This LOI takes effect on the date of the last signature below and remains in effect for the pilot duration in Section 3, unless ended earlier by either Party in writing.
