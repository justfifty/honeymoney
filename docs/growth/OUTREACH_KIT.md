# HoneyMoney — HR Pilot Outreach Kit

Goal: book no-cost pilots with Malaysian employers. Target outcome for MAIC Nexus 2026: **2 signed Letters of Intent**. Everything below is copy-paste ready — swap the `[brackets]` and go.

---

## (a) Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Company shape**
- Malaysian SME / mid-market employers, **80–800 staff** (big enough to matter, small enough to decide fast).
- Sectors with wage earners who feel money stress: **manufacturing, F&B / retail chains, BPO / call centres, logistics, plantations, hospitality, healthcare, shared-services (GBS)**.
- Bonus fit: companies already talking about ESG, employee wellbeing, or fighting high turnover / absenteeism.
- Located in KL/Klang Valley, Penang, Johor (easy to meet), but Telegram delivery means location is not a blocker.

**Who to target (decision-makers & champions)**
- **HR Manager / Head of People / HR Business Partner** — primary buyer.
- **CHRO / HR Director** — for larger orgs.
- **CEO / COO / Founder** in owner-run SMEs (they own the budget).
- **CSR / ESG / Sustainability lead** — wellness is an easy ESG win.

**Trigger signals worth acting on**
- Recently posted about high turnover, retention, or "employer of choice."
- Hiring blitz (churn pain), factory/shift workforce, or B40/M40-heavy payroll.
- Just did an engagement survey, EPF/Retirement talk, or financial-literacy day.

**Disqualify (for now):** <50 staff, no HR function, purely commission gig workforce with no employer relationship.

## (e) Discovery-Call Questions

1. How many staff, and what's the rough profile — office, shift/factory floor, field?
2. Is employee wellbeing or retention on your radar this year? What's driving it?
3. Do you have any financial-wellness or EPF/retirement support today? How's uptake?
4. What does turnover / absenteeism look like — any sense of the cost?
5. Who else would be involved in saying yes to a pilot? (budget, ESG, IT)
6. If we ran a free 60–90 day pilot for 20–50 staff, what would "this worked" look like to you?
7. Any data-privacy or PDPA concerns we should address up front?
8. What's your timeline — could we start something this quarter?

## (f) Objection Handling

**"We don't have budget / what does it cost?"**
> Totally fair — the pilot is **RM 0**. Nothing to pay for 60–90 days, no setup fee, no lock-in. We only talk pricing if the pilot proves its worth to your team, and even then it's a separate conversation. You're risking time, not money.

**"What about data privacy / PDPA?"**
> Built for this. Participation is 100% voluntary and consent-based, fully PDPA-aligned. Individual financial data stays **private to the employee** — as HR you never see anyone's personal spending. You only ever get **anonymised, aggregated** wellbeing insights across the group, and only when the group is big enough that no one can be identified. We don't sell data, full stop.

**"We already have EPF talks / an EAP / other benefits."**
> Great — those are usually one-off sessions or a hotline people forget. HoneyMoney is different: it's **always-on and proactive**, living in an app your staff already use every day (Telegram). It complements EPF/EAP rather than replacing them, and uptake tends to be far higher because there's no class to attend. The pilot is free, so it's easy to see the difference side by side.

**"My staff won't use it / low digital literacy."**
> If they can send a WhatsApp screenshot, they can use HoneyMoney — that's literally the whole interaction. No app to install beyond Telegram, no forms. We handle onboarding for you, and the pilot shows you real uptake numbers before you commit to anything.

**"Send me info and I'll get back to you."**
> Happy to — I'll send a one-pager and the pilot LOI. To make it useful, can I put 15 minutes in the diary next week so I tailor it to [Company] rather than sending generic stuff? Even a quick "not now" on that call saves us both time.

## (g) 5-Touch Follow-up Cadence

| Touch | Day | Channel | Message gist |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | Email (Script 1) | First cold email — the turnover/stress hook |
| 2 | Day 3 | LinkedIn | Connect + short follow-up note |
| 3 | Day 6 | Email reply | Bump the thread: "Floating this back up — worth 15 min?" + attach one-pager |
| 4 | Day 10 | WhatsApp / call | If warm intro or they engaged: short WhatsApp nudge, or a call |
| 5 | Day 16 | Email (break-up) | "Sounds like the timing isn't right — I'll close the loop. If wellbeing comes up later, I'm one message away. Should I check back in Q[x]?" |

**Rules of thumb**
- Stop the moment they reply and book the call — the cadence exists to earn a reply, not to nag.
- Always give an easy out ("a quick no is fine") — it lifts reply rates and keeps goodwill.
- Log every touch and referral. One happy pilot HR manager is your best intro to the next.
- Respect PDPA / anti-spam: only cold-message business contacts on business channels, honour opt-outs immediately.
