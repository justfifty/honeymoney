# HoneyMoney for Employers
### Turn financial stress into financial wellness — as an employee benefit.

**A private AI financial coach for every employee, delivered where they already are:
their phone.** No new app to install. Employees forward an e-wallet or receipt
screenshot to a Telegram bot, and "Honey" — their AI financial guide — does the rest.

---

## The Problem

Financial stress is one of the largest hidden drains on workforce productivity.
Stressed employees are more distracted at work (presenteeism), take more unplanned
leave, and are likelier to leave for a small pay bump. In Malaysia, rising cost of
living and low household savings buffers make this especially acute. Most HR wellness
programs cover physical and mental health — but the *root cause* for many employees is
money.

> *Industry commentary widely links financial stress to reduced productivity and higher
> turnover. Figures in this document are illustrative estimates for pilot planning, not
> guarantees.*

## The Solution — three things HoneyMoney does

- **Effortless tracking.** Forward a screenshot; AI (Google Gemini) extracts the
  transaction; the employee confirms it in one tap. No spreadsheets, no manual entry.
- **A calmer money system.** The 3-Bucket model separates Fixed Non-Negotiables,
  automatic "Future Shield" savings, and private personal wallets — reducing money
  friction, including between partners.
- **Proactive, judgment-free guidance.** Honey nudges toward better habits and answers
  money questions in plain language — private, always-on, and marital-safe.

## What Employees Get vs. What Employers Get

| Employees get | Employers get |
|---|---|
| A private AI money coach on Telegram | An **anonymized, aggregate** workforce financial-health dashboard |
| Effortless expense capture & 3-Bucket budgeting | Insight into wellness trends (e.g. share of staff building an emergency buffer) |
| Automatic savings ("Future Shield") | A differentiated, modern benefit for hiring & retention |
| Judgment-free, marital-safe guidance | A measurable wellness initiative that targets a *root cause* of stress |

**Privacy note:** Employers **never** see an individual employee's transactions,
balances, or personal data. The dashboard shows only anonymized, aggregated trends
across the workforce, and is suppressed for groups too small to anonymize safely.
HoneyMoney operates consistent with Malaysia's PDPA 2010.
