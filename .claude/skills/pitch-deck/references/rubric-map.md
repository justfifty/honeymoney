# MAIC rubric map — every criterion → the slide that proves it (cited)

Grounding for the **pitch-deck** skill. The scoring safety-net: judges score **what they can see** —
hidden merit scores zero. Weights: **Technical 25 · Commercial 25 · Relevance 20 · Scalability 15 ·
ESG/National 15.**

## What judges actually read (design for skim)
- **First read ≈ 3 min 44 sec total — design for skim, not study.** [DocSend/TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2015/06/08/lessons-from-a-study-of-perfect-pitch-decks-vcs-spend-an-average-of-3-minutes-44-seconds-on-them/)
  *(2015 study, widely reported still stable — treat as directional.)*
- **Win the first 3 slides — only ~58% of decks are read to completion.** [DocSend](https://www.docsend.com/pitch-deck-metrics/) ·
  [analysis](https://stradonn.com/2025/05/12/vcs-decide-in-3-minutes-how-to-make-your-pitch-deck-count/)
- **Attention-seconds per slide (funded decks):** Team **62s**, Financials **52s**, Traction **49s**,
  Problem 38s, Solution 33s, Business Model 30s, Market 27s, Competition 22s, Product 21s. → **Invest
  most polish in Team, Financials, Traction; don't over-build the Product slide's text** (put that
  effort into the live/recorded demo). [DocSend via PitchGrade](https://pitchgrade.com/blog/what-investors-read-pitch-deck-docsend-data)
  *(per-slide figures relayed via PitchGrade, not first-party — directional.)*

## Section → criterion map
| Deck section | Primary criterion |
|---|---|
| Problem + local data | Industry Relevance (20) |
| Solution + Product/Demo + "Underlying Magic" (AI) | Technical Feasibility (25) |
| Business Model + Financials + Traction | Commercial Viability (25) |
| Market Size + Go-to-Market + Why Now | Scalability (15) / Commercial |
| ESG / Impact slide (SDG/MADANI/inclusion) | ESG & National (15) |
| Team | credibility multiplier across all (esp. Technical + Commercial) |

## How to make each criterion "scoreable"
- **Technical (25) — front-load it.** A dedicated "how the AI works + why it's real *today*" slide;
  the knowledge-graph model + multi-provider reliability + per-call cost ledger. A live/recorded demo
  is the strongest technical-feasibility evidence. [relativity](https://www.relativity.com/blog/how-to-judge-a-hackathon-4-criteria-to-picking-a-winner/)
- **Commercial (25) — front-load it.** Business-model slide with **unit economics**: price per employer
  seat/month, gross margin (the RM0-cost stack is the margin story), a simple LTV/CAC or payback line,
  and honest traction/pipeline.
- **Relevance (20).** Tie the Problem to Malaysian fintech pain with **local data** (below). T3 is
  explicitly *inclusive finance* — lean in. [MAIC T3](https://maicnexus.com/en)
- **Scalability (15).** A slide showing the growth mechanism: one engine → 3 personas, multi-language/
  multi-market, low marginal AI cost. [taikai](https://taikai.network/en/blog/hackathon-judging)
- **ESG (15) — dedicated, quantified, never a footnote.** SDGs + MADANI + a measurable inclusion KPI.
- **Close with a "rubric echo"** — one line restating impact across all five axes so judges leave with
  the full scorecard. [scorejudge](https://scorejudge.com/blog/posts/how-to-judge-a-startup-pitch-competition/)

## Malaysian evidence to cite (reconfirm against primary BNM before a live slide)
- **47% of Malaysians can't raise RM1,000 for an emergency** (Bank Negara FCI Survey). [summary](https://www.hopesmalaysia.com/post/b40-income-malaysia-2025)
- **70% save under RM500/month; 63% have <3 months' emergency fund** (RinggitPlus 2022 Financial
  Literacy Survey). [summary](https://www.hopesmalaysia.com/post/b40-income-malaysia-2025)
- **Household debt-to-GDP ~84%** — among the region's highest (see `finance-content/references/malaysia-facts.md`
  for the primary BNM citation).
- **FEN: low confidence in financial knowledge = the #1 B40 challenge** — an AI financial-wellness guide
  directly addresses it (defensible impact claim). [FEN via summary](https://www.hopesmalaysia.com/post/b40-income-malaysia-2025)
- **Frameworks to name:** SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 8 (Decent Work & Growth), SDG 10 (Reduced
  Inequalities); **Ekonomi MADANI**; BNM financial-inclusion agenda; MITI **i-ESG** (frames ESG as a
  ~US$12T opportunity — inclusion as a growth wedge protects the Commercial score while winning ESG).
  [MADANI](https://www.investmalaysia.gov.my/resources/latest-announcements/ekonomi-madani-rm120-billion-domestic-direct-investment-boost-from-glics-under-mof-led-programme/) ·
  [SDG Roadmap](https://ekonomi.gov.my/sites/default/files/2024-09/SDG_Roadmap_for_Malaysia_Phase2_(2021-2025)_0.pdf) ·
  [i-ESG](https://www.miti.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/9849)
