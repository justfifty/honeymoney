# Live pitch — delivery + Q&A bank (cited)

Grounding for the **pitch-deck** skill. For the KL semi-final (live demos, "does it work and scale?")
and Grand Final ("is this team investable and ready to commercialise?"). [MAIC](https://maicnexus.com/en)

## Narrative arc
- **Oscillate "what is" ↔ "what could be," ending on "the new bliss."** Contrast pulls judges toward
  your vision. [Duarte/HBR](https://hbr.org/2012/10/structure-your-presentation-li) · [Duarte TED](https://www.duarte.com/resources/talks/the-secret-structure-of-great-talks/)
- **The Malaysian user is the hero; HoneyMoney is the guide with a plan.** Reframes "look how clever our
  AI is" into "here's how a struggling family wins." [Duarte](https://www.duarte.com/blog/presentation-storytelling-audience-is-hero/) ·
  [StoryBrand](https://www.creativeo.co/post/storybrand-framework)
- **Make the problem visceral with one named person before any numbers.** [Duarte](https://mixpanel.com/blog/nancy-duarte-on-how-to-give-a-good-speech/)
- **Give a simple 3-step path** to how the user succeeds (even if the tech under it has 15 steps). [StoryBrand](https://www.gravityglobal.com/blog/complete-guide-storybrand-framework)

## Delivery rules
- **Build for 3–5 min; protect Q&A. Rehearse full / 3-min / elevator versions.** [frankiekemp](https://frankiekemp.com/how-to-master-the-three-minute-pitch-and-avoid-its-pitfalls/) ·
  [allied.vc](https://www.allied.vc/guides/effective-3-minute-investor-video-pitch)
- **Rehearse 15–20×; film yourself** for pacing, filler words, body language. [Qubit](https://qubit.capital/blog/rehearse-pitch-presentation) ·
  [Informi](https://informi.co.uk/starting-business/improve-your-startup-pitch-14-body-language-tips)
- **Hook in the first 15s** — visceral problem or startling local stat, not "Hi, we're team X." [HBR](https://hbr.org/2012/10/structure-your-presentation-li)
- **2-min power pose backstage; refocus nerves onto "what the judges need to hear."** [pitch.com](https://pitch.com/blog/steps-to-delivering-a-standout-pitch)
- **Off-podium; open palms at navel height; no crossed arms.** Hidden body reads negative; open palms
  read trustworthy. [Informi](https://informi.co.uk/starting-business/improve-your-startup-pitch-14-body-language-tips)
- **Vary pitch/tone/volume; deepen the voice; kill uptalk** (rising declaratives signal you don't
  believe your own claims). [Informi](https://informi.co.uk/starting-business/improve-your-startup-pitch-14-body-language-tips)
- **Prefer a live demo, always carry a recorded backup** (Wi-Fi/hardware de-risk). The `demo-video` is
  that backup. [allied.vc](https://www.allied.vc/guides/effective-3-minute-investor-video-pitch)
- **≤2–3 speakers; rehearsed hand-offs; a named Q&A owner per topic.** Ragged transitions read as an
  unrehearsed team. [Qubit](https://qubit.capital/blog/rehearse-pitch-presentation)
- **Visible stopwatch every rehearsal; never run over.** Overrunning gets you cut. [Qubit](https://qubit.capital/blog/rehearse-pitch-presentation)

## Q&A bank — pre-write + rehearse answers to the 6 hardest
1. **Moat / defensibility** — what stops a bank or Setel copying the 3-bucket + graph? (Answer angle:
   the transparency-with-autonomy UX + the graph engine + B2B2C wellness distribution + local-first
   trust; data network effects over time.)
2. **Unit economics** — price per employer seat, gross margin, CAC/payback. Have real numbers, label
   assumptions.
3. **Regulation / licensing** — are you giving financial advice? (Answer: educational-not-advice; not a
   licensed adviser; route to licensed pros + AKPK — see `finance-content` voice-and-compliance.)
4. **Competition** — never "no competitors"; name budgeting apps / bank apps / spreadsheets and your
   wedge.
5. **AI accuracy / hallucination** — how do you keep Honey safe? (Deterministic rule-based fallback +
   graph-grounded context + human-confirms-the-bucket + per-call cost/telemetry ledger.)
6. **Data privacy (PDPA)** — sensitive financial data. (Local-first, tenant isolation, screenshots
   parsed-then-discarded, k-anonymity for corporate roll-ups; PDPA Amendment 2024 obligations known.)

## Mistakes that lose points
"No competitors" claim · top-down TAM · vague ask · feature-dumping / leading with tech & CVs · jargon
+ text-crammed slides · self-serving feature-table competition slide · thin team slide · running over
time · hand-wavy unquantified ESG.
