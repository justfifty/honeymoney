# Demo-video production — recording, audio, captions, retention (cited)

Grounding for the **demo-video** skill. Numbers are pulled verbatim from sources.

## Screen recording
- **Capture at 1080p (1920×1080), 100% scale; go 1440p/4K only for zoom headroom.** [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/screen-recording-to-demo-video) · [TechSmith](https://www.techsmith.com/learn/tutorials/camtasia/crisp-clear-screen-video/)
- **30 fps** is fine for UI with limited motion. [TechSmith](https://support.techsmith.com/hc/en-us/articles/203729508)
- **Prep the stage:** clean desktop, close apps, fix the window to a consistent size, readable zoom,
  **pre-staged demo data**. [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/screen-recording-to-demo-video)
- **Record in 5–10 min segments; reset on any mistake** — don't recover on-camera. [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/screen-recording-to-demo-video)
- **Zoom into the exact button/field; one consistent annotation style** (circle/arrow) for clicks. [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/screen-recording-to-demo-video) · [TechSmith](https://www.techsmith.com/learn/tutorials/camtasia/crisp-clear-screen-video/)
- **Cursor smoothing on; add click/highlight animations.** [TechSmith](https://www.techsmith.com/learn/tutorials/camtasia/crisp-clear-screen-video/)
- **Cut all dead air, thinking pauses, redundant clicks, menu hovering; speed-ramp loading to 1.5–2×.** [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/screen-recording-to-demo-video)
- **Record silent clean B-roll first, then VO over it** (standard for a scripted, tightly-timed piece)
  — but storyboard the visual↔word sync first so post-alignment is trivial. [Crayo](https://crayo.ai/blog/how-to-screen-record-on-mac-with-voiceover) · [TechSmith VO](https://www.techsmith.com/blog/voice-over/)

## Voiceover audio
- **Decent mic, quiet non-echoey room; VO peaks -12 dB to -6 dB.** [Voiceovers.com](https://www.voiceovers.com/blog/best-db-level-for-voice-over)
- **Music 15–20 dB below VO (min -3 to -6 dB under); duck under speech.** [Protunes](https://protunesone.com/blog/top-tips-for-balancing-voiceovers-with-background-music-in-videos/) · [RMM](https://recordmixandmaster.com/2024-05-how-to-mix-voiceovers-and-background-music)
- **Master to YouTube target: integrated -15 to -13 LUFS, true peak ≤ -2 dBTP.** [Sage Audio](https://www.sageaudio.com/articles/mastering-voice-over-dialogue-for-video) · [Descript](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-to-set-the-perfect-audio-levels-for-video)

## Captions (assume muted autoplay + accessibility)
- **Always caption — a video without captions loses >50% of potential audience.** [motionvillee](https://motionvillee.com/15-mistakes-to-avoid-when-creating-a-product-demo-video/) · [Section508](https://www.section508.gov/create/captions-transcripts/)
- **Spec:** ≤2 lines, ≤~42–45 chars/line, white text on semi-transparent black, ≥4.5:1 contrast,
  bottom-center. [Bird Eats Bug](https://birdeatsbug.com/blog/video-captioning-best-practices) · [Section508](https://www.section508.gov/create/captions-transcripts/)
- **Reading speed 120–160 wpm; sync exactly to speech; break lines at natural pauses.** [Bird Eats Bug](https://birdeatsbug.com/blog/video-captioning-best-practices)
- **Never ship raw auto-captions** — edit for accuracy, spell out slang, bracket sound cues. [Bird Eats Bug](https://birdeatsbug.com/blog/video-captioning-best-practices)

## Retention & pacing
- **Win the first 30s** — aim ≥70–75% still watching at 0:30; ~55% drop within the first minute. [YTShark](https://ytshark.com/youtube-audience-retention/) · [Retention Rabbit](https://www.retentionrabbit.com/blog/2025-youtube-audience-retention-benchmark-report)
- **Pattern interrupt / concrete visual within 5s** → ~23% better retention; "losing 35% in first 10s
  rarely recovers." [Retention Rabbit](https://www.retentionrabbit.com/blog/ultimate-guide-youtube-audience-retention) · [Artiphik](https://artiphik.com/blog/the-first-10-seconds-retention-playbook)
- **Front-load the wow** — show the working product early; engagement drops sharply after ~3 min
  (you're at the cap). [motionvillee](https://motionvillee.com/15-mistakes-to-avoid-when-creating-a-product-demo-video/)
- **Cut ruthlessly** — "as short as possible"; a 20-min raw take usually edits to ~6 min, you must be
  far tighter. [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/screen-recording-to-demo-video)
- **One continuous forward-moving narrative;** B-roll/screenshots only to prove points, not to pad. [YTShark](https://ytshark.com/youtube-audience-retention/)

## Showcasing the tech credibly
- **Demo the live app on real data — no mockups.** Authentic footage = credibility. [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/blog/product-demos) · [Vidico](https://vidico.com/news/best-product-demo-video-examples/)
- **One unbroken end-to-end workflow** beats a feature tour (capture → parse → bucket → insight →
  dashboard). [WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script)
- **Reveal the knowledge graph as a quick visual payoff narrated as user benefit, then move on** —
  proves Technical depth without boring non-technical judges. [WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script)
- **Prove impact with 1–2 specific numbers + timeframe, not adjectives.** [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)
- **Optional clean screenshot gallery** (the `/graph` gallery, dashboard) as proof-of-polish B-roll —
  keep buttons/text large; tiny UI doesn't read on a phone. [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)

## CTA & upload
- **End on one action, spoken + on-screen, low friction** ("See it live at honeymoney.app"). [Vidyard](https://www.vidyard.com/blog/end-video-strong-call-action-examples/) · [WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script)
- **End card = logo + one-line pitch + live URL;** restate the 3–4 vertebrae as you land it. [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)
- **Host YouTube Unlisted** (safe default: link-viewable, hidden from search); Vimeo if you need
  password/embed control. **Verify the official MAIC T3 submission spec** (file vs link, host,
  resolution, whether unlisted is allowed) before uploading. [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/blog/post/unlisted-vs-private-videos) · [Lemonlight](https://www.lemonlight.com/blog/youtube-vs-vimeo/)
- **Title + description with keywords** (product name, "MAIC Nexus T3," what it does + live URL +
  repo). [Vimeo SEO](https://vimeo.com/blog/post/vimeo-youtube-video-seo)
