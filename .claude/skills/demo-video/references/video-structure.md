# Demo-video structure — beat sheet + word budget (cited)

Grounding for the **demo-video** skill. HoneyMoney's demo: screen-recording of the live app +
Telegram + VO, **hard cap 3:00**, hitting all five T3 criteria.

## The word budget (your scarcest resource)
- Narration pace **~150 wpm** (130–150 standard; 150–160 = energetic-but-clear). ⇒ **3:00 ≈ 420–450
  words MAX.** Treat words as the constraint. [Bread n Beyond](https://breadnbeyond.com/articles/how-many-words-does-a-60-seconds-explainer-video-needs/) ·
  [Voice Realm](https://www.thevoicerealm.com/count-script.php)
- **Slow to ~100–130 wpm for the knowledge-graph moment** — complex content needs air. [src](https://breadnbeyond.com/articles/how-many-words-does-a-60-seconds-explainer-video-needs/)

## The hook rule (retention is won here)
- **Open on the problem or the payoff in the first sentence — no name, no "welcome," no logo
  animation.** Animated intros >3s cost 8–15% of viewers immediately; 0:00–0:15 is the steepest
  drop-off. [Artiphik](https://artiphik.com/blog/the-first-10-seconds-retention-playbook) · [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)
- **First ~8 seconds is a hard hook deadline; concrete visual within 5s.** [WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script)
- **Don't bury the lead** — if a metric/wow exists, lead with it. [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)

## Canonical beat order
hook → problem → solution/value prop → **live demo (ONE workflow end-to-end)** → proof/impact → CTA.
[WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script) · [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/) · [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/product-demo-best-practices)
Positioning sentence to anchor the solution: **"[Product] helps [specific user] achieve [outcome]
without [frustration]."**

## HoneyMoney beat sheet (3:00 cap, ~430-word budget)
| Timecode | Beat | Secs | Job → T3 criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | **Hook** | 8 | Pain in one sentence + concrete visual (stressed couple / messy receipts). Pattern interrupt ≤5s. → *Relevance* |
| 0:08–0:25 | **Problem** | 17 | Agitate a specific, felt Malaysian household-money scenario. → *Relevance* |
| 0:25–0:38 | **Solution / value prop** | 13 | The positioning sentence; introduce Honey + 3-bucket model. → *Commercial* |
| 0:38–1:45 | **Live demo (one flow)** | ~67 | Real app/data: capture receipt → parse → bucket → Honey insight → dashboard. Zoom each key action; brief graph reveal narrated as *user benefit* (slow VO). → *Technical* |
| 1:45–2:20 | **Impact / business / scale** | 35 | 1–2 concrete metrics + bottom-up market size; one line on how it scales (one engine, 3 personas). → *Commercial + Scalability* |
| 2:20–2:40 | **ESG + relevance close** | 20 | Financial-wellness / inclusion; local-first PDPA; who it helps. → *ESG + Relevance* |
| 2:40–2:55 | **CTA + end card** | 15 | Logo + one-line pitch + **honeymoney.app**; restate the 3–4 "vertebrae." Spoken + on-screen. |
| 2:55–3:00 | **Buffer** | 5 | Hold end card; stay under the cap. |

> You have ~2 min more than a standard 90s demo template — **spend the surplus on the live demo and
> the impact/ESG beats, never on a longer intro.** [WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script)

## Scriptwriting rules
- **Narrate outcomes, not clicks** — let visuals carry mechanics; voice only the non-obvious. [WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script) · [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/product-demo-best-practices)
- **One idea per sentence, plain spoken language** — understandable "in one second," captions cleanly.
  [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)
- **Lead with felt relevance, not clever intrigue.** [WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script)
- **Match every VO line to its on-screen action** (two-column script: word ↔ visual). [WhatAStory](https://www.whatastory.agency/blog/how-to-write-a-product-demo-video-script)
- **Speak unnaturally slowly; pause on key numbers.** [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)
- **Pick 3–4 "vertebrae"** (what/for whom · why now · why hard · why unmissable) and restate at the
  end. [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)
