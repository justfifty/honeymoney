## EPF / KWSP contribution rates
*(effective for Oct 2025 wage / Nov 2025 contribution month)*
- **Malaysian / PR, under 60:** Employee **11%**; Employer **13%** if monthly wages **≤ RM5,000**,
  **12%** if **> RM5,000**. [KWSP Mandatory Contribution](https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/employer/responsibilities/mandatory-contribution)
- **Age 60+:** reported Employee **5.5%**, Employer **4%** — **UNVERIFIED, confirm against the KWSP
  Third Schedule** (60+ rates changed repeatedly). [secondary](https://www.ajobthing.com/resources/blog/epf-kwsp-contribution-rates-2026-how-much-do-employees-employers-contribute)
- **Foreign workers (non-domestic, valid pass, <75):** **2% + 2%**, effective **1 Oct 2025**. [KWSP](https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/employer/responsibilities/mandatory-contribution)
- **Compliance:** for wages < RM20,000 use the **exact Third Schedule amounts**, not a raw %.
  Contributions due by the **15th of the following month**. [Third Schedule PDF](https://www.kwsp.gov.my/documents/d/guest/jadual-ketiga-bi-pdf-1)

## EPF account restructuring *(effective 11 May 2024)*
Every new contribution splits into: **Akaun Persaraan 75%** (locked till 55) · **Akaun Sejahtera 15%**
(housing/health/education, age-50 access) · **Akaun Fleksibel 10%** (withdraw anytime).
[KWSP Account Restructuring](https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/member/account-centre/account-restructuring)
> Content angle: Akaun Fleksibel is a real "flexible" bucket, **but do NOT present it as an emergency-
> fund substitute** — withdrawing depletes retirement.

## SOCSO / PERKESO & EIS
- **SOCSO (Employment Injury + Invalidity, First Category):** Employer ≈ **1.75%**, Employee **0.5%**
  of wages — **table-based**, not flat %. [PERKESO rates](https://www.perkeso.gov.my/en/rate-of-contribution.html)
- **Wage ceiling raised RM5,000 → RM6,000**, effective **1 Oct 2024**. [secondary](https://www.ajobthing.com/resources/blog/perkeso-contribution-table-2025-employers-malaysia)
- **EIS:** **0.2% employer + 0.2% employee = 0.4%**; capped ≈ **RM11.90/party** at the RM6,000 ceiling
  — **exact capped RM UNVERIFIED, confirm on PERKESO table**. [PERKESO contributions](https://www.perkeso.gov.my/en/our-services/employer-employee/contributions.html)
- Both due by the **15th** of the following month.

## Minimum wage
- **RM1,700/month** (up from RM1,500). **Fully effective nationwide 1 Aug 2025** (no more deferments);
  phased from 1 Feb 2025 for employers with ≥5 workers. Excludes domestic workers. [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2025/07/22/minimum-wage-order-at-rm1700-to-be-enforced-nationwide-from-august-1-no-more-deferments-says-ministry/184772)

## Personal income tax (LHDN) — resident individual, YA 2025
⚠️ **Cross-check the full table + every relief cap on [hasil.gov.my](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/individual/individual-life-cycle/income-declaration/tax-rate/)
before publishing** (direct LHDN rate URL 404'd at research time; table below is from a secondary
source).

| Chargeable income (RM) | Marginal rate |
|---|---|
| 0 – 5,000 | 0% |
| 5,001 – 20,000 | 1% |
| 20,001 – 35,000 | 3% |
| 35,001 – 50,000 | 6% |
| 50,001 – 70,000 | 11% |
| 70,001 – 100,000 | 19% |
| 100,001 – 400,000 | 25% |
| 400,001 – 600,000 | 26% |
| 600,001 – 2,000,000 | 28% |
| > 2,000,000 | 30% |
[secondary table](https://www.sql.com.my/income-tax-calculator-malaysia/ya-2025/)

- **PCB (Potongan Cukai Bulanan / MTD):** monthly tax deducted at source by the employer — an
  installment toward annual tax, **not a separate tax**. (Verify wording against LHDN.)
- **Chargeable income = Gross − EPF (capped) − reliefs**, then apply brackets.
- **Key reliefs (YA 2025) — confirm each sub-limit on hasil.gov.my:** Individual **RM9,000** (auto);
  non-working spouse **RM4,000**; child under 18 **RM2,000** each; disabled (OKU) individual
  **+RM6,000**; **rebate RM400 if chargeable income ≤ RM35,000**. [LHDN reliefs](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/individual/individual-life-cycle/income-declaration/tax-reliefs/)

## AKPK — free help (use for distressed-user referrals)
- **AKPK** = a BNM subsidiary; all core services **free**. Hotline **03-2616 7766**, [akpk.org.my](https://www.akpk.org.my/).
- Three services: financial education; free **one-on-one counselling**; **Debt Management Programme
  (DMP)**. [AKPK](https://www.akpk.org.my/debt-management) · [DMP](https://www.akpk.org.my/debt-management-programme)
- **DMP:** AKPK negotiates with all lenders → one consolidated monthly repayment, possibly reduced
  interest. Needs NRIC + proof of income. [guide](https://money.com.my/guides/akpk-debt-management-malaysia)
- **Honest caveat:** DMP enrolment **flags restructured facilities on CCRIS** — better than default,
  but not free of record. State this when recommending it.

## PDPA — financial-data privacy (product requirement, not just a footer)
- **Personal Data Protection Act 2010**, materially updated by the **Amendment Act 2024** (headline
  obligations effective **1 June 2025**); 7 data-protection principles. [DataGuidance](https://www.dataguidance.com/opinion/malaysia-personal-data-protection-amendment-act)
- **Breach notification:** notify the Commissioner **within 72 hours** of awareness; notify affected
  individuals if significant harm likely. [DLA Piper](https://privacymatters.dlapiper.com/2025/03/malaysia-guidelines-issued-on-data-breach-notification-and-data-protection-officer-appointment/)
- **Mandatory DPO** for large-scale processing (Malay+English, resident/contactable in MY). [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2025/07/from-legislative-reform-to-practical-guidance-key-amendments-to-malaysias-pdpa-and-the-launch-of-cross-border-transfer-guidelines)
- **Penalties** up to **RM1,000,000** + 3 years' imprisonment; **biometric = sensitive data**; new
  **data-portability** right. [DataGuidance](https://www.dataguidance.com/opinion/malaysia-personal-data-protection-amendment-act)
- Implication: as controller of sensitive household financial data, HoneyMoney likely needs a DPO,
  a 72h breach process, explicit consent, minimisation, and portability. Only state what the app
  actually does. See `voice-and-compliance.md`.
